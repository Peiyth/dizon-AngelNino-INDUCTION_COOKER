02 / SAFETY FIRST

Dos & Don'ts

Good habits make a better cooking experience.

✓

DO

  • Use induction-compatible cookware.
  • Place the cooker on a flat, dry surface.
  • Keep the ventilation openings unobstructed.
  • Clean the surface after it has cooled.
  • Unplug the cooker when not in use.
  • Follow the manufacturer's instructions.
✕

DON'T

  • Don't use cookware that is not induction-compatible.
  • Don't touch the cooking surface immediately after use.
  • Don't immerse the cooker in water.
  • Don't block the air vents.
  • Don't operate with a damaged power cord.
  • Don't leave cooking unattended.

Important: Always refer to the official product manual for specific operating instructions and safety precautions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about INDUCTION COOKER Series 01.

What is an induction cooker?

An induction cooker uses electromagnetic technology to heat compatible cookware directly instead of heating the cooking surface itself.

What cookware can I use?

You need induction-compatible cookware, usually made from magnetic stainless steel or cast iron.

Is induction Cooker easy to use?

Yes! The touch controls and digital display are designed to make adjusting cooking settings simple.

Is the induction cooker safe?

Induction cooking generally heats cookware directly. Always follow the product instructions, use compatible cookware, and keep the surface clean and dry.

How do I clean the cooker?

Turn off and unplug the cooker. Allow it to cool, then wipe the surface with a soft damp cloth. Avoid abrasive cleaners.

Where can I learn how to use it?
